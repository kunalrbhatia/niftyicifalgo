### Description

Implements full scrip master fallback resolution and 24-hour disk caching in `scripMasterResolver.ts` so that equity positions (e.g. `ABB`, `RELIANCE`) correctly resolve their NSE cash equity spot instrument tokens when the local `scrip_master.json` is NFO-only.

### Changes

- Added `downloadFullScripMaster(cachePath, ttlHours)` in `strategy-brain/src/scripMasterResolver.ts` with a 24h disk cache (`data/full-scrip-master.json`) to avoid repeated 37MB downloads.
- Added `resolveSpotTokenWithFallback(underlying)` in `strategy-brain/src/scripMasterResolver.ts` to check NIFTY special case $\rightarrow$ local master $\rightarrow$ full master fallback.
- Updated `fetchSpot(jwt?, underlying?, spotToken?)` in `strategy-brain/src/broker.ts` to accept pre-resolved spot tokens.
- Updated `SitrepCollector.buildFromBroker()` in `strategy-brain/src/sitrep.ts` to use `resolveSpotTokenWithFallback()`.
- Added `FULL_SCRIP_MASTER_PATH` and `FULL_SCRIP_MASTER_TTL_HOURS` to `strategy-brain/src/config.ts` and `.env.example`.
- Added `data/full-scrip-master.json` to `strategy-brain/.gitignore`.
- Added unit tests in `strategy-brain/test/scripMasterResolver.test.ts` and `strategy-brain/test/broker.test.ts`.
- Updated `README.md` documentation.
