### Description

Fixes per-underlying spot lookup in Strategy Brain so equity option positions (such as ABB or RELIANCE) fetch and evaluate against their actual equity spot prices instead of hardcoding the NIFTY index spot price. Candidate adjustment legs and strikes are also scaled and labeled according to the active underlying.

### Changes

- Added `strategy-brain/src/scripMasterResolver.ts` with `resolveSpotTokenFromScripMaster()` and `loadScripMaster()` to resolve NSE cash equity spot tokens from `scrip_master.json` and map NIFTY index to `99926000`.
- Parameterized `fetchSpot(jwt?, underlying = 'NIFTY')` in `strategy-brain/src/broker.ts` to fetch equity spot quotes dynamically via SmartAPI `/getLtpData`.
- Updated `SitrepCollector.buildFromBroker()` in `strategy-brain/src/sitrep.ts` to derive the underlying from `STRATEGY_PREFIX` or open positions and record `spotUnderlying` and `approximateSpot` in `marketContext`.
- Updated `strategy-brain/src/research.ts` to construct candidate adjustment legs and strike intervals scaled to the target underlying.
- Enhanced cycle header logging in `strategy-brain/src/orchestrator.ts` to display `(underlying: ABB, spot: ...)`.
- Added unit tests in `strategy-brain/test/scripMasterResolver.test.ts` and extended `strategy-brain/test/broker.test.ts`.
- Updated `README.md` with the new scrip master resolver and spot lookup functionality.
